--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable shallow woven wicker basket with a broad rim, a few simple woven bands, two short side handles and ONE small cartoon fish tail poking over the rim, as a standalone 2D children's storybook object. Warm muted tan and brown wicker, soft watercolor grain, gentle painted shading, rounded softened silhouette. Centered with comfortable padding, reads clearly at small size. It must read as a fish basket, not a bowl, bucket or plain tray.

Match the Little Play painted storybook style of the attached house and basket references: soft watercolor wash, subtle paper grain, warm muted colours, no heavy outline.

CRITICAL: the object inside the basket must be a small fish (a simple fish shape with a tail fin poking over the rim), NOT a star, NOT a cookie, NOT any other shape. The fish is the subject.

Exclusions:
- No glossy 3D render, photo-realism, vector emoji, heavy black outline, glow
- No face, eyes, person, hand, letters, text, logo, watermark
- No extra baskets, no clutter, no background scene
- No star-shaped objects, no cookie shapes, no fruit, no toys, no cloth shapes other than a plain cloth lining
- One fish only
