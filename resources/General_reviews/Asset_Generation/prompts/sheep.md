--- prompt ---

Create one standalone illustration of a friendly sheep for a cozy early-childhood storybook game. Match the supplied Little Play bear and golden star references: soft watercolor wash with very subtle paper/colored-pencil grain; warm natural, gently muted colors; rounded, slightly organic shapes; soft painted shading; restrained contrast; quiet handmade charm; minimal, clean details; readable silhouette at small mobile size. Draw one single full-body sheep standing calmly, three-quarter view toward the viewer, with a clearly readable rounded wool silhouette in muted warm off-white and cream wool, a simple warm-grey and beige face and legs, small dark calm eyes, and a soft closed mouth. Keep the wool a simple rounded mass with only a few soft lobes instead of hundreds of tiny curls, so the shape stays clean and readable at card size. Use a calm friendly expression, no exaggerated cartoon grin. One subject only, fully visible, centered with comfortable breathing room. Plain warm off-white background is acceptable. No added props, ground line, or scene unless requested. The result must look like it belongs beside the exact reference images.

No photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy plastic, vector emoji, stock-icon look, or anime styling. No thick black outline, white sticker border, glow, hard drop shadow, or dramatic lighting. No excessive gradient, tiny detail, heavy texture, decorative sparkles, or ornamental extras. No unrequested accessories. No text, labels, watermark, logo, collage, contact sheet, or multiple alternatives in one image. Do not reproduce the reference bear or the golden star; create the requested sheep in the same visual family.
