--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable simple children's telescope as a standalone storybook illustration: a refractor telescope on a tripod, angled up toward the sky.

- One long tube made of **two or three stepped barrel segments**: a WIDE front barrel at the upper end and a narrow rear barrel that steps down toward the viewer, so the tube visibly tapers from front to back like a real telescope.
- At the upper front opening show a **visible round glass lens** — a smooth pale blue-white disc with one soft crescent highlight, seated inside a slightly wider front rim, clearly enclosed by the barrel.
- At the lower rear end show a **small narrow eyepiece** — a short cylindrical tube with a dark round opening, much thinner than the front barrel.
- A simple mount between tube and tripod, with a **three-legged tripod** standing evenly on a level base.

Use muted warm brown, cream and gentle blue-grey, soft watercolor grain, rounded construction and gentle shading. Center the complete telescope with comfortable padding. It must read as a real telescope, not a camera, a spyglass held by a person, a trumpet, or a plain tube with no lens.

No person, hands, stars, sky, planets, text, logo, face, scene, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, emoji/vector styling, heavy black outline, glow, or extra objects.
