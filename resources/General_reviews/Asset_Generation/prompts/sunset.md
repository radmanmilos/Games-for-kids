--- prompt ---

Create one simple sunset as a standalone children's storybook illustration: a **sun setting between low mountains**.

- **Low mountains:** two or three low, soft, rounded mountain silhouettes along the bottom, in muted blue-grey and warm earthy tones, with a gentle saddle between them. No snow, no trees, no buildings.
- **Sun:** a warm orange-red half-disc (or slightly more) sitting in the saddle between the low mountains, just above the peaks, with a soft warm glow. The sun is a simple disc, not a star, not a sparkle, not a burst.
- Soft watercolor grain, warm orange, pink and muted blue-grey tones, in the Little Play family style with no heavy outline.

Center the whole scene with comfortable padding and no cropping. The sun and the low mountains are the only subjects. It must read as a sunset between low mountains, not a sunrise, not a star, not a sparkle and not an abstract glow.

No star, no star shape, no sparkle, no burst, no straight rays, no pointed spikes, no bear, no person, no trees, no buildings, no clouds, no sky details, no text, logo, face, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, emoji/vector styling, heavy outline, strong glow or extra suns.
