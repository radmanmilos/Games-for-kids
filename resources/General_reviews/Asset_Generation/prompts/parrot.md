# object-parrot — generation brief

Replaces: `🦜` in dino and racing tropical collectible/scenery uses.

Inventory row: `object-parrot` — shared parrot asset.

Why: Tropical gameplay needs one shared standalone parrot illustration; no matching WIP parrot asset exists.

Category: animal / bird.

References: `bear.png` (canonical painted style anchor), `star.png` (canonical paper/warmth reference). Use them for rendering style only; do not copy their subjects or colours. `bird.png` was dropped in iteration 2 (user feedback: iteration 1 read as a generic bird/canary). Iteration 3 follows the user's decision: model the subject on the **Scarlet macaw (Ara macao)**, the picture children already recognise as "папига".

Canvas: square 1024×1024 WIP image, one centered object with generous padding.

Background: warm cream storybook paper, softly painted and unobtrusive; no transparency required for this WIP candidate.

--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable simple Scarlet macaw (Ara macao) as a standalone children's storybook illustration — the iconic default parrot a child instantly names "папига". The species pattern must be unmistakable at a glance, even shrunk to card size:

- Bright scarlet red covering the head, chest, belly and most of the body.
- A bare white face patch around the eye, plain and simple, with one small calm dark eye inside it.
- A large, deep, strongly HOOKED beak: ivory-pale upper mandible curving down over a darker lower one, big relative to the head (roughly a quarter of the head).
- A yellow band across the upper wing, with deep blue wing tips and blue long tail feathers at the end — the classic red/yellow/blue macaw wing order from body outward.
- LONG pointed scarlet tail feathers that extend clearly past the feet and taper to a blue tip.
- An upright, chest-forward posture with a rounded body, as macaws sit — not a small horizontal songbird.
- Simple gripped feet with two toes forward and two back.

Match the Little Play family style: rounded softened construction, restrained watercolor grain, muted storybook red rather than neon, gentle painted shading, and no heavy outline.

Center the complete macaw with comfortable padding and no cropping. The macaw is the only subject. It must read as a Scarlet macaw at first glance — not a canary, finch, generic songbird, chicken, duck, owl, toucan, or a plain green parrot.

No green body, no small pointed beak, no short stubby tail, no horizontal songbird posture, no detailed feather-by-feather rendering, no cage, branch, perch, jungle, leaves, flowers, multiple birds, person, hand, text, letters, logo, exaggerated glossy eye, face, background scene, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, emoji/vector styling, heavy black outline, strong glow, or extra objects.
