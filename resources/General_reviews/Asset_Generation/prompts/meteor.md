Create one clearly recognizable simple meteor as a standalone children's storybook illustration.

- One rounded warm grey-brown rock head with a short broad tapering tail trailing behind, in muted blue-grey and warm golden tones. The head has a soft highlight; the tail fades to a point.
- Soft watercolor grain, rounded softened painted edges, gentle shading, no strong motion lines.
- Muted colour, readable at small size, in the Little Play family style with no heavy outline.

Center the meteor with comfortable padding and no cropping. It is the only subject. It must read as a meteor, not a comet with a long tail, a shooting star or a fireball.
