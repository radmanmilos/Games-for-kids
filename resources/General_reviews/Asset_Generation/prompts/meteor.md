--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable simple meteor as a standalone children's storybook illustration: an **irregular gray rock with small craters**, no tail.

- **Body:** one irregular, lumpy gray rock (warm grey-brown to cool grey), with a couple of small round craters (slightly darker shallow pits with a soft rim) on its surface, and gentle watercolor grain. Rounded softened edges but an uneven, lumpy outline — not a smooth sphere, not a perfect oval.
- **No tail:** the rock stands alone, with no trail, streak or tail behind it. Just the stone.
- A small soft cast shadow beneath, low contrast, in the Little Play family style with no heavy outline.

Center the rock with comfortable padding and no cropping. It is the only subject. It must read as an irregular cratered gray meteorite, not a comet with a tail, a shooting star, a fireball or a smooth ball.

No tail, no streak, no trail, no pointed rays, no star shape, no smooth sphere, no scene, text, logo, face, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, emoji/vector styling, heavy outline, glow or extra meteors.
