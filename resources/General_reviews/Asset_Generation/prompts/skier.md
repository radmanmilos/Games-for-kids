--- prompt ---

Create one simple friendly child skier as a standalone children's storybook illustration, with correct anatomy and correct ski equipment.

- One small fully visible child seen from the side in a calm, stable skiing stance, knees slightly bent, leaning gently forward.
- **Body count:** exactly one head with a rounded hat, one body in a muted winter jacket, **two arms**, **two legs** in muted trousers, **two boots**. No third leg, no extra arm, no duplicated boot.
- **One ski per foot:** each boot stands on **its own separate ski**, so exactly **two skis** are visible, side by side front-to-back in perspective — never both feet on one ski, never a leg standing on the snow.
- **Two poles, one in each hand:** exactly **two ski poles**, each gripped by one hand and angled back toward the ground. Two sticks, not one, and not a pole shared between both hands.
- The face stays minimal and calm (two dot eyes and a small smile at most), proportions gentle, soft watercolor grain, no dramatic action.

Center the whole figure with comfortable padding and no cropping. The child is the only subject. It must read as a child skiing with two short skis and two poles.

No third leg, no extra arm, no duplicated boot, no both feet on one ski, no missing ski, no single pole, no extra ski, no mountain, snow scene, other people, race, text, logo, exaggerated face, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, emoji/vector styling, heavy outline, glow or extra equipment.
