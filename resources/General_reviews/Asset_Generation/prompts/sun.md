--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable simple sun as a standalone children's storybook illustration, built around a **soft corona** rather than straight rays.

- **Corona:** a warm golden-yellow circular disc in the centre, surrounded by a **soft, hazy corona** — a gentle, slightly irregular ring of paler golden light that fades outward into the paper, like a soft glow halo. No straight spikes, no pointed rays, no zig-zag bursts.
- The disc itself has a subtle warm highlight on the upper left and a slightly deeper golden tone toward the lower right, with soft watercolor grain.
- Keep the whole sun simple, round and readable at small size, in the Little Play family style with no heavy outline.

Center the sun with comfortable padding and no cropping. It is the only subject. It must read as a sun with a soft corona, not a star, a sparkle, a flower or a fried egg.

No straight rays, no pointed spikes, no zig-zag bursts, no star shape, no face, eyes, smile, sky, clouds, landscape, text, logo, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, emoji/vector styling, heavy outline, strong glow or extra suns.
