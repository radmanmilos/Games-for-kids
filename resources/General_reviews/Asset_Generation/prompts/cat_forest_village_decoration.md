--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable terracotta flower pot planted with a few simple cheerful flowers and small green leaves, as a standalone 2D children's storybook decoration. Warm muted terracotta pot with a soft rim, rounded softened silhouette, a couple of simple painted blossoms in warm pink, soft yellow and lavender, gentle green leaves. Soft watercolor grain, gentle painted shading, no heavy outline. Centered with comfortable padding, reads clearly at small size.

Match the Little Play painted storybook style of the attached house and potted-plant references: soft watercolor wash, subtle paper grain, warm muted colours, rounded organic shapes.

Exclusions:
- No glossy 3D render, photo-realism, vector emoji, heavy black outline, glow
- No face, eyes, person, hand, letters, text, logo, watermark
- No extra pots, no clutter, no background scene, no sky, no ground line
- Keep it a single pot with a handful of flowers, not a bouquet or a garden bed
