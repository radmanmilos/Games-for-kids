# object-moon — generation brief

Replaces: `🌙` in classroom, driving, dino, ocean, space, fastcat and racing collectible/theme uses; existing 3D and procedural moon geometry remains separate runtime rendering.

Inventory row: `object-moon` — shared moon illustration for gameplay objects; classroom time-toggle UI may remain an icon.

Why: Emoji moon uses need one shared standalone raster illustration; no matching WIP moon asset exists.

Category: simple space/nature object.

References: `bear.png` (canonical painted style anchor), `star.png` (canonical paper/warmth reference), `bone.png` (approved simple-object form and detail level). Use these for rendering style only; do not copy their subject shapes.

Canvas: square 1024×1024 WIP image, one centered object with generous padding.

Background: warm cream storybook paper, softly painted and unobtrusive; no transparency required for this WIP candidate.

Iteration note (2026-10-29): iteration 1 was a broad crescent moon. User feedback: it reads as a young moon — they want a **full moon with a few craters**, closer to the real moon. Iteration 2 delivered the full moon and was approved on shape, but the user then asked for the colour to be **more yellow-grey** — iteration 3 changes only the colour.

--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable simple FULL moon as a standalone children's storybook illustration: a single complete round disc, not a crescent and not a partly lit sphere.

Show the full circle of the moon in a muted **yellow-grey** — a warm dusty ochre tint dulled with mid grey, like a real moon seen through daylight: not bright cream, not pure white, not orange, not gold. Add a small number of simple soft craters — four to six rounded, shallow, slightly darker warm-grey patches of different sizes, spread gently across the disc, one larger near the upper left. Keep the craters soft and painted, not detailed or photographic, and keep the disc evenly and fully lit (no shadow side, no terminator line).

Match the Little Play family style: rounded softened construction, restrained watercolor grain, gentle painted edge and surface shading, muted but unmistakable colour, and no heavy outline. The disc must stay readable at small game-object size.

Center the complete full moon with comfortable padding and no cropping. The moon is the only subject. It must read as a full moon, not a crescent, a ball, an egg, a cookie, or a cheese wheel.

No crescent shape, no partly lit sphere, no shadow side, no face, eyes, smile, stars, sky, clouds, planet, rocket, astronaut, text, letters, logo, background scene, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, emoji/vector styling, heavy black outline, strong glow, or extra moons.
