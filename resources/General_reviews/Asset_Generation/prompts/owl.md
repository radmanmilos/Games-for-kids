Create one standalone illustration of a single simple owl for a cozy early-childhood storybook game. Match the supplied Little Play bear, golden star and bird references: soft watercolor wash with very subtle paper/colored-pencil grain; warm natural, gently muted colors; rounded, slightly organic shapes; soft painted shading; restrained contrast; quiet handmade charm; minimal, clean details; readable silhouette at small mobile size.
