--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable simple rock as a standalone children's storybook illustration.

- One rounded, slightly irregular rock in warm grey-brown, with a couple of soft painted planes (lighter top, darker underside) and gentle watercolor grain. Rounded softened edges, no sharp facets.
- A small soft cast shadow beneath, low contrast.
- Muted colour, readable at small size, in the Little Play family style with no heavy outline.

Center the rock with comfortable padding and no cropping. It is the only subject. It must read as a rock, not a boulder pile, a mountain or a potato.

No mountain, boulder pile, gravel, soil, grass, text, logo, face, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, emoji/vector styling, heavy outline, glow or extra rocks.
