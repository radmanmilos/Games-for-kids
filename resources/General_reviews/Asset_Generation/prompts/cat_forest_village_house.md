--- prompt ---

Create one standalone illustration of a cozy woodland cottage for a toddler storybook game. Match the supplied Little Play bear and golden star references: soft watercolor wash with very subtle paper/colored-pencil grain; warm natural, gently muted colors; rounded, slightly organic shapes; soft painted shading; restrained contrast; quiet handmade charm; minimal, clean details; readable silhouette at small mobile size. Keep the subject architecturally recognizable and visually balanced, with a calm friendly feeling. No faces or personification. One subject only (the cottage and its immediate garden clearing), fully visible, centered with comfortable breathing room. Transparent background is NOT required for this scene background; a plain warm off-white paper background is acceptable.
