--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable standard jigsaw puzzle piece as a standalone children's storybook illustration, shown **tilted in 3D space**.

- **One colour only:** the whole piece is a single solid muted colour (a warm mid-blue or a soft coral red), with gentle painted shading to suggest a slightly tilted 3D plane. No patterns, no picture, no multi-colour.
- **Standard jigsaw shape:** one classic interlocking piece with one tab on top, one blank on the right, one tab on the bottom, one blank on the left (or any standard single-tab/single-blank arrangement), with smooth rounded edges.
- **Tilted in 3D:** the piece is rotated a little and tilted away from the viewer, so it reads as a flat piece floating in 3D space at a slight angle, with a soft cast shadow beneath it. Not lying flat, not face-on.

Soft watercolor grain, rounded softened painted edges, in the Little Play family style with no heavy outline.

Center the piece with comfortable padding and no cropping. It is the only subject. It must read as a single tilted standard jigsaw piece in one colour, not a flat icon, not a board and not a 2D silhouette.

No multiple pieces, no picture, no letters, numbers, text, logo, face, board, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, emoji/vector styling, heavy outline, strong glow or extra objects.
