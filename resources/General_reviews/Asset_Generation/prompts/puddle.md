--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable simple puddle as a standalone children's storybook illustration that **feels like a real puddle on the ground**, not a plate.

- **Puddle feel:** an irregular, organic pool of water sitting in a shallow dip in the ground — wavy, uneven edges, not a smooth oval or a perfect circle. The water is pale blue-grey with soft reflections (a lighter streak or two) and a few small concentric ripple marks. The ground around it is warm cream paper with a faint darker edge where the water meets the earth.
- A couple of tiny splash droplets or a wet sheen just outside the rim, to sell the wetness. Soft painted, low contrast.
- Muted colour, readable at small size, in the Little Play family style with no heavy outline.

Center the puddle with comfortable padding and no cropping. It is the only subject. It must read as a real puddle on the ground, not a plate, a lake, a mirror or a top-down circle.

No plate, no perfect oval, no smooth rim, no lake, river, rain, person, boot, reflection of a scene, text, logo, face, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, emoji/vector styling, heavy outline, glow or extra puddles.
