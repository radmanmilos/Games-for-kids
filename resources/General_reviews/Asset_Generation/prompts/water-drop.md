The tone instructions are now explicit instead of "muted": a clear, unmistakable water blue with the blue visibly stronger than the red, a ban on grey-blue / slate / steel, and the elephant reference is told to supply the *coolness* only, not its muted blue-grey.
- **References sent to the model:** `Reference/bear.png` (canonical style anchor), `Reference/star.png` (canonical secondary reference), `elephant.png` (painted family — the cool tone only, never its shape). --- prompt ---

Create one standalone illustration of a single simple water drop for a cozy early-childhood storybook game. Match the supplied Little Play bear, golden star and elephant references: soft watercolor wash with very subtle paper/colored-pencil grain; warm natural, gently muted colors; rounded, slightly organic shapes; soft painted shading; restrained contrast; quiet handmade charm; minimal, clean details; readable silhouette at small mobile size.

Draw one plump water drop standing upright on its rounded bottom: a smooth teardrop shape, wide and heavy at the base, tapering to a soft rounded point at the top, slightly asymmetric so it looks hand-drawn.

Fill it with a **clear, unmistakable water blue**. The blue must be obvious and definite: a clean daylight pool-and-sky blue with the blue clearly, visibly stronger than the red in it — the red content low, the blue high. It must NOT read as grey, grey-blue, slate, steel, dusty or washed out. If the picture comes out grey or beige it is wrong. Keep it a soft painted blue rather than a harsh one, but never mute it into grey: think of clean water in sunlight, and let the warm off-white background stay warm so the blue clearly stands apart from it.

Paint one soft lighter highlight on the upper left side and a slightly deeper blue shading along the lower right edge, so the drop reads as a rounded, glossy surface without looking shiny or plastic. The drop must carry a clearly visible amount of tone so it still reads when the picture is shrunk to a small card.

Keep it a simple, calm object picture with no face, no eyes and no expression. The drop must read instantly as a water drop at small size and must not look like a bird, an egg, a teardrop-shaped leaf, a balloon or a blob.

One subject only, fully visible, centered with comfortable breathing room. Plain warm off-white background is acceptable. No added props, no puddle spreading under it, no glass, no tap, no sink, no splash, no ripples, no ground line, no other drops. The result must look like it belongs beside the exact reference images.

No photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy plastic, vector emoji, stock-icon look, or anime styling. Not a grey drop, not a grey-blue drop, not slate, not steel, not a yellow drop, not a green drop, not a rain cloud, not a water splash, not a crying face, not a balloon, not a drop of blood. No thick black outline, white sticker border, glow, hard drop shadow, or dramatic lighting. No excessive gradient, tiny detail, heavy texture, decorative sparkles, or ornamental extras. No unrequested accessories. No text, labels, watermark, logo, collage, contact sheet, or multiple alternatives in one image. Do not reproduce the reference bear, the golden star or the elephant; create the requested water drop in the same visual family.
