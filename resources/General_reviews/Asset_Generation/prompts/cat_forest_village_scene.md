--- prompt ---

Create one standalone illustration of the Cat Forest village screen layout for a 2D toddler storybook game. Gentle three-quarter / front-facing diorama view. The cozy woodland cottage is the dominant landmark, centered-left. Warm honey wood walls, cream plaster, muted terracotta roof. A visible front porch/covered area with THREE clear fixed feeding positions (small plate outlines or mat outlines). A short curved path leads from the house to a pond hotspot (lower-right, water visible through trees) and a woodland gathering hotspot (upper-right, fallen logs). Garden surrounds the house with decoration anchor points (empty flower-pot rings on the ground). Workshop visible as a small shed/lean-to on the side. Diary/book object visible on a small outdoor table near the door. Background: peaceful forest clearing, distant trees, soft hills, gentle sky. Layered: distant forest → middle-ground trees/buildings → foreground grass/flowers. Soft contact shadows, consistent light from upper left. Palette: forest deep muted pine/deep teal; foliage sage/moss/soft fern; house warm honey wood, cream plaster, muted terracotta roof; ground warm grass green with muted yellow highlights; pond dusty blue. Rounded silhouettes, gentle asymmetry, subtle paper grain, soft edges, hand-painted edges never harsh outlines. Cozy, readable shapes at small tablet sizes. Negative space around all touch targets (house, 3 feeding positions, pond hotspot, woodland hotspot, workshop, diary).

Exclusions:
- No photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy plastic, vector emoji, stock-icon look, or anime styling
- No thick black outline, white sticker border, glow, hard drop shadow, or dramatic lighting
- No excessive gradient, tiny detail, heavy texture, decorative sparkles, or ornamental extras
- No unrequested face, eyes, smile, cheeks, or accessories on the house/objects
- No text, labels, watermark, logo, collage, contact sheet, or multiple alternatives in one image
- Do not copy Secret Cat Forest furniture or character art (IP rule)
- No aggressive angles, extreme perspective, or dramatic foreshortening
- Do not crop off roof, porch, chimney, or identifying parts
- Keep negative space around touch targets (plan §5.3)
