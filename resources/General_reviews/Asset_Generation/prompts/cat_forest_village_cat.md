Phase 1 art-direction prototype for the Cat Forest expansion. Establishes the cat identity style (shape, palette, rendering finish) that all ten cats will follow (plan §3.1, §5). Not a replacement of an existing emoji — a purpose-made prototype.

--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable friendly tabby cat sitting upright, viewed at a gentle three-quarter angle for a 2D toddler storybook game. Soft orange-brown tabby stripes over cream fur, round softened head, small rounded ears, calm gentle eyes, one single long curled tail (never two). Comfortable padding, subject centered and fully visible, reads clearly at small size.

Match the Little Play painted storybook style used by the attached house and cat references: soft watercolor wash, subtle paper grain, warm muted colours, rounded organic shapes, soft painted shading, no heavy outline. Sits calmly, friendly and huggable.

Exclusions:
- No photo-realism, 3D render, glossy plastic, vector emoji, anime styling
- No M-shaped or letter-like forehead marking, no text, no collar with letters
- No second tail, no double tail
- No background scene, no props, no multiple cats
- No thick black outline, sticker border, glow or hard shadow
- No person, hand, logo, watermark or collage
