--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable simple comet as a standalone children's storybook illustration.

- The comet head is a **single, almost perfectly round ball** — a clear circle, only very slightly flattened, with a soft painted highlight on the upper left. It is a solid object, not a streak, not a sparkle and not a burst.
- A **broad tapering tail flows out behind the head to one side**, starting as wide as the head and narrowing to a soft rounded point. The tail is clearly attached to the head and clearly behind it, like motion through space — not a flame, not a fan of separate rays.
- The tail carries two soft colour bands: a pale blue-grey outer band and a warm pale golden inner band, painted with restrained watercolor grain.

Keep the whole comet (round head plus broad tail) simple, wide and readable at small game-object size, in the Little Play family style: rounded softened construction, muted but unmistakable colour, gentle painted shading and no heavy outline.

Center the complete comet with comfortable padding and no cropping. The comet is the only subject. It must read as a round comet with a tail, not a shooting star, star with rays, fireball, sparkle, tadpole or a ball with a separate rag.

No star shape, no pointed rays, no star-shaped head, no narrow thread tail, no separate floating pieces, no sky, stars, planets, rocket, smoke, face, text, logo, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, emoji/vector styling, heavy outline, strong glow or extra objects.
