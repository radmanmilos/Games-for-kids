--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable simple mountain as a standalone children's storybook illustration: **a single mountain body that carries a couple of peaks on top**.

- **One continuous mountain**: one broad shared base and one continuous rock mass, with the peak line rising into **two (or three) summits on the same body**, close together. The tallest summit stands near the centre, the second summit is clearly lower and narrower right beside it, and they are joined across a shallow shoulder — like a hand with two raised fingers, not two separate cones standing apart.
- No gap, no valley and no separate base cutting the mass in two: the rock fills the whole silhouette from base to peaks.
- A small pale snow cap on the tallest summit and a smaller snow patch on the second summit.
- Two or three softly painted earthy blue-grey planes across the faces, gentle watercolor grain, rounded softened edges.

Keep the silhouette simple, wide and readable at small scenery size, in the Little Play family style, with no heavy outline.

Center the whole mountain with comfortable padding and no cropping. It is the only subject. It must read as **one mountain with a couple of peaks** — not a single sharp triangle, not two mountains side by side, not a volcano and not a long range.

No two separate mountains, no wide valley between peaks, no mountain range, four or more peaks, sky, clouds, sun, trees, houses, people, road, snow field, text, logo, scene, glossy 3D rendering, photorealism, emoji/vector styling, heavy outline, glow or extra objects.
