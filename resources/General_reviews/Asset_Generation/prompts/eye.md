--- prompt ---

Create one standalone illustration of a single simple human eye for a cozy early-childhood storybook game. Match the supplied Little Play bear, golden star and bone references: soft watercolor wash with very subtle paper/colored-pencil grain; warm natural, gently muted colors; rounded, slightly organic shapes; soft painted shading; restrained contrast; quiet handmade charm; minimal, clean details; readable silhouette at small mobile size.

Draw one simple eye: an almond-shaped eye with a coloured iris and a dark pupil, and a few simple eyelashes on the upper lid. Keep the silhouette compact and instantly readable as an eye.

Paint the iris a soft, warm brown (or gentle blue-green), the pupil a soft dark brown/black, and the white (sclera) a soft warm cream. Paint one soft lighter highlight and a slightly deeper shading so the eye reads as rounded and dimensional without looking glossy or 3D. Keep the colors clearly readable when the picture is shrunk to a small card.

Keep it a simple, calm object picture with no face, no other facial features. No eyebrow, no tears, no glasses. The eye must read instantly as an eye at small size and must not look like a leaf, a oval or a blob.

One subject only, fully visible, centered with comfortable breathing room. Plain warm off-white background is acceptable. No added props, no ground line, no other object. The result must look like it belongs beside the exact reference images.

No photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy plastic, vector emoji, stock-icon look, or anime styling. Not a leaf, not an oval, not a blob. No thick black outline, white sticker border, glow, hard drop shadow, or dramatic lighting. No excessive gradient, tiny detail, heavy texture, decorative sparkles, or ornamental extras. No unrequested face or accessories. No text, labels, watermark, logo, collage, contact sheet, or multiple alternatives in one image. Do not reproduce the reference bear, the golden star or the bone; create the requested eye in the same visual family.
