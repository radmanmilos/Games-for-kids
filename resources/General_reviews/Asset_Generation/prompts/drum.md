--- prompt ---

Create one standalone illustration of a single simple drum for a cozy early-childhood storybook game. Match the supplied Little Play bear, golden star and bone references: soft watercolor wash with very subtle paper/colored-pencil grain; warm natural, gently muted colors; rounded, slightly organic shapes; soft painted shading; restrained contrast; quiet handmade charm; minimal, clean details; readable silhouette at small mobile size.

Draw one simple drum: a short cylindrical body with a circular drumhead on top and a circular drumhead on the bottom, like a small hand drum or tom-tom. Keep the silhouette compact and instantly readable as a drum.

Paint the drum body a soft, warm brown (or muted terracotta), and the drumheads a soft, warm cream/off-white. Add a few simple, soft lacing lines around the sides where the heads meet the body. Paint one soft lighter highlight and a slightly deeper shading so the drum reads as rounded and dimensional without looking glossy or 3D. Keep the colors clearly readable when the picture is shrunk to a small card.

Keep it a simple, calm object picture with no face, no eyes and no expression. No drumsticks, no hands, no stand, no extra drums. The drum must read instantly as a drum at small size and must not look like a can, a barrel or a cylinder.

One subject only, fully visible, centered with comfortable breathing room. Plain warm off-white background is acceptable. No added props, no ground line, no other object. The result must look like it belongs beside the exact reference images.

No photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy plastic, vector emoji, stock-icon look, or anime styling. Not a can, not a barrel, not a cylinder. No thick black outline, white sticker border, glow, hard drop shadow, or dramatic lighting. No excessive gradient, tiny detail, heavy texture, decorative sparkles, or ornamental extras. No unrequested face or accessories. No text, labels, watermark, logo, collage, contact sheet, or multiple alternatives in one image. Do not reproduce the reference bear, the golden star or the bone; create the requested drum in the same visual family.
