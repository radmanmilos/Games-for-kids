Create one standalone illustration of a single simple child (student) for a cozy early-childhood storybook game. Match the supplied Little Play bear, golden star and bone references: soft watercolor wash with very subtle paper/colored-pencil grain; warm natural, gently muted colors; rounded, slightly organic shapes; soft painted shading; restrained contrast; quiet handmade charm; minimal, clean details; readable silhouette at small mobile size.

Keep it a simple, calm picture with no extra props beyond a small book or backpack. No graduation cap, no gown, no diploma. The child must read instantly as a young student at small size and must not look like an adult or a doll.

One subject only, fully visible, centered with comfortable breathing room. Plain warm off-white background is acceptable. No added props beyond a small book, no ground line, no other person. The result must look like it belongs beside the exact reference images.

No thick black outline, white sticker border, glow, hard drop shadow, or dramatic lighting. No excessive gradient, tiny detail, heavy texture, decorative sparkles, or ornamental extras.
