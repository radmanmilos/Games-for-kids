--- prompt ---

Create one standalone illustration of a single simple strawberry for a cozy early-childhood storybook game. Match the supplied Little Play bear, golden star and bone references: soft watercolor wash with very subtle paper/colored-pencil grain; warm natural, gently muted colors; rounded, slightly organic shapes; soft painted shading; restrained contrast; quiet handmade charm; minimal, clean details; readable silhouette at small mobile size.

Draw one strawberry as a plump, slightly heart-shaped berry: rounded and full, tapering to a gentle point at the bottom, with a small cluster of short green leaves (the calyx) sitting at the top like a little crown. Keep the silhouette compact and instantly readable as a strawberry.

Fill the berry with a clear, unmistakable strawberry red — a warm, soft, slightly deep red, clearly red and never brown, orange, pink, muddy or grey. The red must be obvious and definite. Paint one soft lighter highlight on the upper left side and a slightly deeper red shading along the lower right edge, so the berry reads as rounded and dimensional without looking glossy, waxy or wet. Keep the color clearly readable when the picture is shrunk to a small card.

Scatter a modest number of small, simple, pale-yellow seeds (achenes) across the surface — a few, clearly spaced, not dense — painted as tiny soft dots that sit on the skin rather than carved into it. Paint the calyx leaves a soft, muted leaf green with simple shapes and no intricate veining.

Keep it a simple, calm object picture with no face, no eyes and no expression. The strawberry must read instantly as a strawberry at small size and must not look like a heart, a tomato, a red apple or a red blob.

One subject only, fully visible, centered with comfortable breathing room. Plain warm off-white background is acceptable. No added props, no stem in hand, no plate, no basket, no other berries, no ground line. The result must look like it belongs beside the exact reference images.

No photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy plastic, vector emoji, stock-icon look, or anime styling. Not a brown strawberry, not an orange strawberry, not a pink strawberry, not a green strawberry, not a tomato. No thick black outline, white sticker border, glow, hard drop shadow, or dramatic lighting. No excessive gradient, tiny detail, heavy texture, decorative sparkles, or ornamental extras. No unrequested face or accessories. No text, labels, watermark, logo, collage, contact sheet, or multiple alternatives in one image. Do not reproduce the reference bear, the golden star or the bone; create the requested strawberry in the same visual family.
