--- prompt ---

Create one standalone illustration of a single simple rabbit for a cozy early-childhood storybook game. Match the supplied Little Play bear, golden star and fox references: soft watercolor wash with very subtle paper/colored-pencil grain; warm natural, gently muted colors; rounded, slightly organic shapes; soft painted shading; restrained contrast; quiet handmade charm; minimal, clean details; readable silhouette at small mobile size.

Draw one rabbit sitting in a calm, friendly pose: a rounded body, a small head, long upright ears, a tiny nose, small paws, and a small fluffy tail. Keep the silhouette compact and instantly readable as a rabbit — the long ears are the key identifier.

Cover the body with a soft, warm brownish-grey/brown — a gentle, muted brown (or soft grey-brown), clearly brown-grey and never orange, black, or muddy. Paint the ears with a slightly lighter inner tone. Keep the face gentle: a small dark eye, a simple tiny nose, no exaggerated expression. The rabbit must read instantly as a rabbit at small size and must not look like a hare, a cat or a brown blob.

Keep it a simple, calm animal picture with no face beyond the gentle expression, no accessories, no props. One subject only, fully visible, centered with comfortable breathing room. Plain warm off-white background is acceptable. No added props, no ground line, no other animal. The result must look like it belongs beside the exact reference images.

No photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy plastic, vector emoji, stock-icon look, or anime styling. Not a hare, not a cat, not a generic brown animal. No thick black outline, white sticker border, glow, hard drop shadow, or dramatic lighting. No excessive gradient, tiny detail, heavy texture, decorative sparkles, or ornamental extras. No unrequested face or accessories. No text, labels, watermark, logo, collage, contact sheet, or multiple alternatives in one image. Do not reproduce the reference bear, the golden star or the fox; create the requested rabbit in the same visual family.
