--- prompt ---

Create one clearly recognizable traffic cone as a standalone children's storybook illustration suitable for a road obstacle in the driving game. Match the Little Play painted storybook style from car.webp and star.png references: soft watercolor wash with very subtle paper/colored-pencil grain; warm natural, gently muted colors; rounded, slightly organic shapes; soft painted shading; restrained contrast; quiet handmade charm.

- One small, friendly traffic cone seen from the side, standing upright on its base. Conical body tapering upward to a rounded point, a couple of soft painted planes (lighter on one side, darker on the other) and gentle watercolor grain. Two soft horizontal stripes (warm orange-red and cream) painted around the body. Rounded softened edges, no sharp facets, no thick black outline.
- Muted warm colors: soft orange-red body with cream stripes, warm brown base shadow.
- Single subject centered with comfortable padding on all four sides. No road, no background, no other objects, no text, no UI.
- Must read as a traffic cone at small size when scaled down to fit ~60px canvas width.

**CRITICAL: The cone must stand UPRIGHT — wide base at the BOTTOM, narrow point at the TOP. Not lying on its side.**
