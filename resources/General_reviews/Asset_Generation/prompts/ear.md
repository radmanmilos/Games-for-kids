--- prompt ---

Create one standalone illustration of a single simple human ear for a cozy early-childhood storybook game. Match the supplied Little Play bear, golden star and bone references: soft watercolor wash with very subtle paper/colored-pencil grain; warm natural, gently muted colors; rounded, slightly organic shapes; soft painted shading; restrained contrast; quiet handmade charm; minimal, clean details; readable silhouette at small mobile size.

Draw one simple ear: a soft, rounded outer ear (the pinna) with a gentle curve and a small inner fold, like a friendly storybook ear. Keep the silhouette compact and instantly readable as an ear.

Paint it a soft, warm light skin tone — a gentle, muted warm tone, clearly an ear and never grey, orange, or muddy. Paint one soft lighter highlight and a slightly deeper shading so the ear reads as rounded and dimensional without looking glossy or 3D. Keep the color clearly readable when the picture is shrunk to a small card.

Keep it a simple, calm object picture with no face, no other facial features. No earring, no hair. The ear must read instantly as an ear at small size and must not look like a leaf, a shell or a blob.

One subject only, fully visible, centered with comfortable breathing room. Plain warm off-white background is acceptable. No added props, no ground line, no other object. The result must look like it belongs beside the exact reference images.

No photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy plastic, vector emoji, stock-icon look, or anime styling. Not a leaf, not a shell, not a blob. No thick black outline, white sticker border, glow, hard drop shadow, or dramatic lighting. No excessive gradient, tiny detail, heavy texture, decorative sparkles, or ornamental extras. No unrequested face or accessories. No text, labels, watermark, logo, collage, contact sheet, or multiple alternatives in one image. Do not reproduce the reference bear, the golden star or the bone; create the requested ear in the same visual family.
