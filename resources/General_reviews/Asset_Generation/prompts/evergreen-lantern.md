- Soft warm painted glow: a gentle warm radiance around the candle inside the glass, low contrast, no rays and no strong halo.

- Gentle watercolor grain, muted but warm colours, no heavy outline — a painted object from the Little Play set, not a metal product shot.

Center the complete lantern with comfortable padding and no cropping. It is the only subject. It must read as a European box lantern holding a lit candle.
