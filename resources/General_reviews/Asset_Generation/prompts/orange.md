--- prompt ---

Create one standalone illustration of a single simple orange for a cozy early-childhood storybook game. Match the supplied Little Play bear, golden star and bone references: soft watercolor wash with very subtle paper/colored-pencil grain; warm natural, gently muted colors; rounded, slightly organic shapes; soft painted shading; restrained contrast; quiet handmade charm; minimal, clean details; readable silhouette at small mobile size.

Draw one orange as a plump, slightly flattened sphere: round and full, with a very subtle dimple at the top where a short stem and one or two small simple leaves sit. Keep the silhouette compact and instantly readable as an orange.

Fill it with a clear, unmistakable orange — a warm, soft, slightly deep orange, clearly orange and never brown, red, yellow, or muddy. The orange must be obvious and definite. Paint one soft lighter highlight on the upper left side and a slightly deeper orange-amber shading along the lower right edge, so the fruit reads as rounded and dimensional without looking glossy, waxy or wet. Keep the color clearly readable when the picture is shrunk to a small card.

Paint the stem and the one or two leaves a soft, muted leaf green with simple shapes and no intricate veining. Keep the leaves small so they do not compete with the fruit. Do not paint individual skin pores or texture; keep the surface softly painted and clean.

Keep it a simple, calm object picture with no face, no eyes and no expression. The orange must read instantly as an orange at small size and must not look like an apple, a pumpkin, a ball or an orange blob.

One subject only, fully visible, centered with comfortable breathing room. Plain warm off-white background is acceptable. No added props, no plate, no knife, no other fruit, no ground line. The result must look like it belongs beside the exact reference images.

No photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy plastic, vector emoji, stock-icon look, or anime styling. Not a red orange, not a yellow orange, not a brown orange, not a pumpkin. No thick black outline, white sticker border, glow, hard drop shadow, or dramatic lighting. No excessive gradient, tiny detail, heavy texture, decorative sparkles, or ornamental extras. No unrequested face or accessories. No text, labels, watermark, logo, collage, contact sheet, or multiple alternatives in one image. Do not reproduce the reference bear, the golden star or the bone; create the requested orange in the same visual family.
